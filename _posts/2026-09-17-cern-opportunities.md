The NTUA Career Office is organizing an event on **internship, research, employment and educational opportunities at CERN** for students and young graduates from all engineering fields.

**Wednesday, 7 October 2026**
**Events Hall, Administration Building, NTUA Zografou Campus**

Representatives from **NTUA and CERN**, as well as NTUA graduates with experience at CERN, will present available opportunities and share their experiences.

The event will be addressed by **NTUA Rector, Professor I. Chatjigeorgiou**.

To attend the event, you must register in advance [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdltlXH-9uaiEI0JwX-xurmhOzY-XTBPANzSK72tALo5i7NbQ/viewform?usp=header).

View the event programme [here](https://career-net.ntua.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PROGRAMMA-F.pdf).
