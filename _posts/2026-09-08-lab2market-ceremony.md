We are delighted to congratulate CalmaPulse, developed by Evgenia Skouloudi, Lamprini Kourou, and Filoxeni-Kyriaki Laskaridou, on winning the Audience Choice Award at the Lab to Market Pitch Competition 2026. The team is developing a real-time solution for stress and anxiety management, designed to help users regulate panic attacks and chronic stress.

The competition, held on July 16, 2026, at the Athens Conservatoire, brought together 15 early-stage teams participating in the inaugural Lab to Market: Bridging Innovation and Entrepreneurship initiative. The program connects Columbia University, the National Technical University of Athens, and Greece’s innovation ecosystem, supporting young researchers and engineers in transforming promising ideas into viable and scalable ventures.

CalmaPulse was originally developed within the Innovation track of NTUA TEAM, reflecting its focus on translating engineering and biomedical knowledge into solutions with real-world impact.

Following months of entrepreneurship training, mentoring, and customer discovery, the team presented its solution to a panel of judges and a live audience. The Audience Choice Award, determined by attendees of the final pitch competition, highlights the relevance of the challenge CalmaPulse addresses and the potential of its solution.

We warmly congratulate Evgenia, Lamprini, and Filoxeni-Kyriaki on this achievement and wish the CalmaPulse team continued success as they develop their venture.
