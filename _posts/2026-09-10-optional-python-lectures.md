Dear students,

we would like to inform you about the opportunity to attend a small number of introductory lectures on Python, which will be one of the programming languages you will be using during your studies. These lectures will take place on Monday 14/09, Wednesday 16/09,  Thursday 17/09, Monday 21/09, Wednesday 23/09 and Thursday 24/09. 

All lectures will be held in Room 008 of the New Building of the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, from 17:00 to 20:00, with the exception of the lecture on Wednesday, September 16, which will be held from 18:00 to 21:00 p.m.

Please note that attendance of these lectures is "not" mandatory. Students who will not be able to attend these lectures will be provided with learning material (in Greek) and links to pre-recorded lectures (in Greek) on the topics that will be covered.

The lectures will be given by Mr Nikolaos Papaspyrou, Professor and Ms Zoe Pαraskevopoulou, Assistant Professor at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering of NTUA.

Best wishes for a great start!
